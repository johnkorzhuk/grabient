Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Grabient ("Grabient," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares personal information when you use grabient.com, its API and MCP server, and the emails we send (the "Service"). Grabient is responsible for the personal information described here.

2. Information we collect

You can use Grabient without an account. If you create one, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture on your Google account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Profile and preferences: your display name and avatar if you add them, your language, whether your display name is shown on palettes, and a record of your newsletter choices and the issues we sent you.
  • Your content: the palettes you save, your collections, and your votes on palettes and searches.
  • Sign-in and app records: a record for each browser you're signed in on, including its name and operating system, and the AI apps you connect through the MCP server, with the access you gave them.
  • Referral information: if you created your account while marketing was on, the website or campaign link that referred you.

Whether or not you have an account, we also collect:

  • Request data: like any website, we receive your IP address and browser information with each request. We use them to deliver the Service, keep it secure, prevent abuse and apply the right privacy settings for your region. We don't store your IP address with your account.
  • Visit statistics: our servers record visits with general information such as the page, your country, your type of device and browser, your network provider, the website or campaign that referred you, and whether you used a palette or searched. To count unique visitors, we create a code from your IP address and browser using a key that changes every day and is then deleted, so the code can't be linked back to you or across days. No cookies are used for this, and your IP address isn't stored. In some countries we count visits without this code, and if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we only count the visit.
  • Searches and palette use: what you search for, and how public palettes and collections are opened, copied, downloaded and edited, counted without recording who you are. Search words we don't recognize may be interpreted by an AI service, without your IP address or account.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form or by email, including your email address if you give it.
  • Error logs: technical records of errors on our servers or in your browser.

3. What others can see

  • Palettes you save appear in the public gallery, with how many times they've been saved. Your display name and account aren't shown with them unless you turn on Your display name on palettes in Settings, which shows it on the palettes you liked first, on Grabient and through its API and MCP server. It's off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Grabient's admins are shown by display name on the palettes they add.
  • Otherwise, your profile, collections, votes and saved palettes are visible only to you.
  • An AI app you connect can see your name and display name, and can see and change your saved palettes and collections, until you disconnect it. Its own privacy policy covers what it does with that information.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to provide and protect the Service: to sign you in, save your content, send account emails, answer messages, prevent abuse, count visits, understand how the Service is used, and rank and curate the gallery based on how palettes are saved and used, without looking at who did what. We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

If you have an account, we may also send you a newsletter about new palettes and features, about once a month. Where the law requires your consent, such as in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, accounts created since this policy took effect receive it only after it's turned on in Settings. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every issue or in Settings.

If you're in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or another place whose law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide your account, your saved content and the emails that come with them.
  • Legitimate interests: to run, secure and improve the Service, including request data, error logs, messages, search, visit statistics, counts of palette use and ranking, and, where the newsletter starts on, to tell people what's new. You can object or opt out at any time.
  • Consent: for showing your display name on palettes, and for the newsletter where it starts off. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the Service:

  • Cloudflare hosts the Service and its data, protects it from attacks, provides AI features such as interpreting searches and checking display names, and delivers account emails and contact messages. Cloudflare's privacy policy
  • Google provides sign-in with Google. Google's privacy policy
  • Resend delivers the newsletter. Resend's privacy policy

We may also disclose information when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety or rights. If Grabient is transferred to someone else, your information may be transferred with it, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

6. Cookies

We use only the cookies and browser storage the Service needs to work: to keep you signed in, remember your settings, keep a copy of what you save so the site works offline, and help protect the site from abuse. We don't use cookies for analytics or advertising, and visit statistics use no cookies or browser storage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, although blocking the ones the Service needs will sign you out.

7. How long we keep information

  • Your account and content: until you delete them or your account.
  • Sign-in records: until you sign out, or they expire after a period without use.
  • Connected apps: until you disconnect the app or delete your account, or a year after the app last used it.
  • Newsletter records: until you delete your account.
  • Logs and other operational records: up to 90 days.
  • Visit statistics: up to three months. Each day's key is deleted when the day ends, after which no statistic can be linked to an IP address.
  • Earlier analytics: what Google Analytics and PostHog collected until October 2026 is deleted automatically within 14 months.
  • Messages: as long as we need them to respond and follow up.
  • Backups: deleted information may remain in backups for up to 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This deletes your account and everything saved with it. Palettes you saved stay in the public gallery, with nothing linking them to you.

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your information in a portable format, or ask us to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw your consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

To make a request, contact us (section 13). We may ask you to confirm it from the email address on your account. We respond within one month. If we can't do what you ask, we'll explain why, and you can appeal by replying. Exercising your rights never affects the service you receive.

9. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections and single-use sign-in links and codes. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

10. International transfers

Our service providers process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, information from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland is transferred under safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

11. Children

The Service isn't intended for children under 13. You must be at least 13 to create an account, or older where the law of your country requires. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with a new date and, if a change is significant, we'll also let you know on the site.

13. Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, email hello@grabient.com or use the contact form.
