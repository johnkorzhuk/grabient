## Generated palette names

`{start} to {end}` is `{start} → {end}` ("Світло-жовтий → рожевий"). Ukrainian would say "від світло-жовтого до рожевого",
but the color names are translated once, in the nominative, and also stand alone when both ends are the same color, so
they cannot take the genitive. The arrow keeps the direction; the About page already writes ranges that way. Color
names are masculine adjectives, as a color is named on its own (синій, світло-жовтий, темно-сірий). Each English name
has its own word, so two never read alike: navy is темно-синій, dark blue густо-синій.

Where a name meets a noun, the name goes in « » after it, so nothing has to agree with it: Палітра «{name}»,
Градієнт «{name}», Зразки «{name}», Палітри, схожі на «{name}». A page title puts the name first:
"{name}: лінійний градієнт". Search words ({subject}, {query}) are treated the same way.

A reader's own title follows the same rule and never sits directly after a preposition, where it would have to
agree. Put a noun in front of it instead: "Added to {title}." is Додано до колекції «{title}», and "Saved from
{title}" Збережена з колекції {title}.

## Register

- The reader is ви, lowercase, as in Google's and Apple's Ukrainian.
- Buttons, menu items and key labels are infinitives: Зберегти, Копіювати, Видалити, Скасувати, Показати більше.
- A search field's placeholder is a noun, not an infinitive: Пошук палітр, Пошук колекцій, as Gmail's Пошук у пошті
  and Google Drive's Пошук на Диску do. Buttons stay infinitives.
- Instructions and hints are imperatives in the ви form: Виберіть, Натисніть, Спробуйте ще раз.
- A tooltip is the press's name, as on its button, then its key: Розвернути · E.
- Statuses and toasts are short impersonal forms: Збережено, Палітру видалено, Шукаємо палітри….
- "X could not be done" is Не вдалося + infinitive: Не вдалося зберегти зміни.
- Quotes are « »; the apostrophe is ’ (U+2019), always literal in the message format.
- No em dashes, and a spaced hyphen is never a dash either: title and subject separators use an en dash (–) or
  « | Grabient», as the page titles do. Where Ukrainian grammar wants a dash between subject and predicate
  ("Точка — це колір"), the sentence is rephrased ("Точка задає колір"). The ranked title that has one in English
  keeps it ("Grabient — генератор…").
- Plurals give all four forms. `other` is the fractional form, which takes the genitive singular: one 1 палітра,
  few 2 палітри, many 5 палітр, other 1,5 палітри. After до, не більше and similar words every form is genitive.
- Numbers in prose use the decimal comma (0,86), as Intl writes them on the same pages; units: 5 МБ, px.
- і / й and у / в follow the euphony rule where it reads naturally (кроки й кут, кут і стиль).
