# Français (fr)

How French says Grabient's own words. Read `README.md` for what each word means here; use the word below every time,
and add a row when a new product word gets its translation.

## The words

| English | Français | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grabient | Grabient | Never translated. |

| gradient | dégradé (m.) | "CSS gradient" is *dégradé CSS*. "CSS gradient generator" in a title is *générateur de dégradé CSS* (the search term, singular). |
| swatches | aplats | The hard-band styles in titles and card names: *aplats linéaires*, *aplats angulaires*, *aplats radiaux*, *Aplats {name}*. The style menu's English says "bands", so there it is *Bandes linéaires / angulaires / radiales*. Not *nuancier*, which is Adobe's panel of saved swatches, not a way of drawing. |

Color names (`colornames.data.ts`) are whole phrases: *bleu canard* (teal), *violet* (purple), *rose* (pink), *bleu
marine* (navy), *bordeaux* (maroon), *fauve* (tan), *marron* (brown), *vert olive* (olive), *blanc cassé* (off-white);
*foncé*, *clair* and *pâle* follow the color (*bleu foncé*, *jaune pâle*). Two ends are `{start} à {end}` (*jaune pâle à
rose*). A palette's title is `{name} en {style}` (*Jaune pâle à rose en dégradé linéaire*), since the name arrives
capitalized and cannot follow the style.

Names of formats and standards stay as they are: OKLCH, OKLab, sRGB, CSS, SVG, PNG, JSON, GLSL, WGSL, Shadertoy, Figma,
MCP, URL, hex codes.

## Register

- *vous* everywhere.
- Buttons, menu items and tooltips that name an action are infinitives: *Copier*, *Retirer de la sélection*,
  *Se connecter*, *Tout réinitialiser*. Instructions and hints to the reader use the imperative: *Collez un lien…*,
  *Essayez…*, *Connectez-vous pour…*. Status lines use a past participle, masculine by default (*Copié*, *Enregistré*,
  *Envoyé*) unless the sentence names its subject (*Palette supprimée.*).
- French typography: a narrow no-break space (U+202F) before `:` `;` `?` `!` and inside « »; the typographic
  apostrophe ’ everywhere (it also keeps the message format from reading `'` before `{` or `<` as a quote); the
  ellipsis … attached (*Chargement…*); U+202F before `%` and as the thousands separator (*5 000*); a no-break space
  between a number and its unit (*5 Mo*, *30 s*); degrees attached (*360°*); a decimal comma in prose (*0,86*), while
  code and values the app prints keep theirs.
- Sentence case for titles and headings. No em dashes, except the home page title, whose
  English keeps "Grabient — …".
- Plurals: French *one* covers 0 and 1 (*0 palette*); *many* is the millions, written with *de* (*# de palettes*).
- The free search's vocabulary is English, so the example words stay English (*navy, cozy ou sunset*) and the
  sentence says *en anglais*.
- Labels stay short: the layout was drawn for the English lengths. *Vider*, *Tout voir (24)*, *Retour en haut*,
  *Défiler vers le bas*; "Press again to delete" is *Confirmer la suppression*, the usual French second step.
