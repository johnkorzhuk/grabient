# Spanish (es)

Neutral international Spanish that reads naturally in Latin America and in Spain (most readers are in Latin America).
The reader is addressed as **tú**. Read the English list in `README.md` first; this file is how Spanish says each word.

Color names (from `colorname.ts`): negro, blanco, gris, rojo, naranja, amarillo, verde, verde azulado (teal), azul,
morado (purple), rosa (pink), durazno (peach, as Mexico says it); "dark X" is "X oscuro", "light X" is "X claro". "{start} to {end}" is
"de {start} a {end}", so a palette is "De amarillo claro a rosa". Where the name sits in a longer phrase the sentence is
built around it: "{name} en {style}" ("De amarillo claro a rosa en degradado lineal"), "{name}: paleta con 7 colores",
"Paletas similares a la paleta {name}".

## Register

- tú throughout.
- Neutral vocabulary that Latin American readers expect and Spain reads without friction: agregar, configuración,
  administrar, presionar (keys and buttons), correo, marrón, rosa, morado. No voseo, no vosotros, no regional slang.
- Sentence case. No exclamation marks, no em dashes: where the English has one, Spanish uses a colon. Opening ¿ and ¡
  where a sentence needs them.
- Buttons, tabs and chips as short as Spanish allows: a verb or a noun ("Guardar", "Copiar", "Al azar"). Status words
  that agree with a collection are feminine ("Privada", "Guardada"); the curation chips agree with the palette they
  judge ("Turbia", "Apagada", "Muy oscura").
- Where a label names a value, the value comes after a colon so its gender and case never clash:
  "{label}: color", "Paletas guardadas: {query}", "En ascenso: 1 año".
- A colon never follows a preposition (RAE): "{key} ya está en uso: {action}", not "ya se usa para: {action}".
- Numbers written by the code keep the decimal point (L 0.86); four-digit numbers in prose have no separator
  (2048, 5000). Units take a space: 5 MB, 100 %.
- Page titles and descriptions use what Spanish searchers type: "generador de degradados", "degradado CSS",
  "paleta de colores". The home title is "Grabient: generador de degradados CSS y paletas de colores".
