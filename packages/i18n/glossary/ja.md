# Japanese (ja)

How Grabient's own words are said in Japanese. Read `README.md` for what each word means, then use the word here every
time. The terms follow what Japanese designers see in Figma, Adobe and CSS references (MDN), and the color science terms
follow JIS Z 8105.

The palettes' generated names (`colorname.ts`) use the everyday color words, with 明るい and 暗い for light and dark
(lightness is 明度, so the pair reads as the same axis) and 淡い for pale: 黒, 白, グレー, 赤, オレンジ, 黄色, 緑,
青緑 (teal), 青, 紫, ピンク, 茶色; 明るい青, 暗い青, 淡い黄色. "{start} to {end}" is {start}から{end}, so a title reads
淡い黄色からピンクの線形グラデーション.

## Register

- Sentences (statuses, errors, descriptions, About) use です・ます. Tooltips are names, like the buttons. A toast says
  what happened: パレットを削除しました。 An error says what failed and what to do: 保存できませんでした。もう一度お試しください。
  "Try again later" is しばらくしてからもう一度お試しください。
- An empty search suggests with 〜で検索してみてください, not the literal 〜で試してください.
- Buttons, tabs, chips and menu items are a noun or a verb stem, as short as the English: コピー, 保存, 書き出し, 削除,
  閉じる, 選択に追加. Work in progress is 〜中…: 保存中…, 検索中….
- Full-width punctuation: 。、「」（）： and …. A list of three short nouns may use the middle dot ・ (スタイル・色数・角度).
  The separator " · " between parts of a label is kept as in the English. No exclamation marks, no em dashes.
- A half-width space goes between Latin words or code and Japanese (CSS をコピー, Google でログイン, {format} をダウンロード),
  never next to full-width punctuation. A number attaches to its counter or unit (3日前, 12件, 7色, 100文字,
  2026年8月15日); a number that is a value or an ID is spaced like a Latin word (キーフレーム 3, L は 0.86).
- A language with one plural form writes an ICU plural as a plain `{count}` ({count}文字以内にしてください);
  `check` accepts this, so don't "fix" it back into plural syntax.
- Relative ages add 前 (3日前), since a bare 3日 reads as the 3rd of the month.
- A reader's search words and a collection's title sit in 「」 inside a sentence (「{title}」を開く) or leading a
  compound title (「{subject}」のグラデーションとカラーパレット, never glued to の as {subject}の…); a page title puts
  them after a colon (保存したパレット：{query}). A palette's generated name needs no brackets ({name}のパレット).
- The words the free search knows are English, so examples a reader is invited to type stay English.
- Page titles end with " | Grabient", the home page's included (CSS グラデーションジェネレーターとカラーパレット | Grabient).
  Titles and descriptions use the words Japanese searchers type: CSS グラデーション, グラデーションジェネレーター,
  カラーパレット, 配色.
- On the About page a drawn strip is グラデーション, never 帯, which is kept for the swatch styles.
- Long vowels are written out: エディター, ユーザー, ブラウザー, シェーダー, パラメーター.
