# 简体中文 (zh-Hans)

How Simplified Chinese says Grabient's own words. Read `README.md` for what each word means here; use the word below
every time, and add a row when a new product word gets its translation.

Color names (`colorname.ts`) are whole phrases with 色: 红色, 深红色, 浅红色 ... 蓝绿色 (teal), 青色 (cyan, CMYK's 青), 靛蓝色 (indigo), 紫色, 粉色,
黑色, 白色, 灰色, 深灰色, 浅灰色. Peach is 蜜桃色: 桃色 alone also means scandal (桃色新闻). Two ends are `{start}到{end}` (浅黄色到粉色); in a label, `{name}的调色板` keeps
"粉色调色板" from reading as 粉色调 + 色板.

Names of formats and standards stay as they are: OKLCH, OKLab, sRGB, CSS, SVG, PNG, JSON, GLSL, WGSL, Shadertoy, Figma,
MCP, URL, hex codes, key names.

## Register

- 你, never 您, everywhere: the English is plain, not
  formal.
- Full-width punctuation: ，。：；？！（）. Quotes are “ ” (single level ‘ ’ inside). The ellipsis in a status line is a
  single … as in mainland interfaces (正在搜索…).
- A half-width space between Chinese and Latin letters or digits: CSS 渐变, 导出 SVG 和 PNG, 第 2 页, 5 分钟. No space
  next to full-width punctuation.
- A reader's own words inside a sentence (a collection's title, a search's words, a user's name) sit in “ ”, as mainland
  interfaces do: 已添加到“{title}”, “{subject}”渐变与配色方案. A translated phrase (a color name) sits in the
  sentence with no space: 类似{name}的调色板.
- A relative age is written with 前, as the Chinese web writes it: 3 天前, 5 分钟前.
- Buttons, tabs and chips as short as the language allows: a verb or a noun (复制, 导出, 已选), no 请 on a button. Error
  lines, toasts, prompts and emails are whole sentences ending in 。, with 请 before an instruction to the reader
  (请重试, 请选择…, 请粘贴…), as mainland interfaces write it.
- A thing that couldn't be done starts with 无法 (无法保存。, 无法加载合集。); Try again is 请重试。, Try again later
  请稍后再试。
- Page titles keep the English shape (`热门渐变配色 | Grabient`, `Grabient — CSS 渐变色生成器与配色方案`) and use the search
  words 渐变色生成器, 配色方案, 渐变配色, CSS 渐变.

## Notes for the next translator

- A short English label that leans on its context (a button beside others, a nav link beside a logo) loses the head
  noun Chinese needs if carried over word for word: 角度渐变 not 角度, 联系我们 not 联系.
- Chinese has one plural form, so a plural is `{count, plural, other {# 个调色板}}`. A plural whose cases carry tags
  (`<n>#</n>`) may write them once, in `other`: the check compares tags by name.
- The examples of search words stay in English (比如 navy、cozy 或 sunset): the free search reads English words, and a
  signed-out reader who types the Chinese gets nothing.
- `{name}` in a palette's title is a translated color phrase (浅黄色到粉色), so `{name}{style}` is written with no space:
  浅黄色到粉色线性渐变.
